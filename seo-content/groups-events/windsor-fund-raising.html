How fundraising at AeroSports Windsor works

Our team works with your group to set up a fundraising event built around jump time at our Windsor location. You promote the event to your community, supporters come out to play, and your group benefits from the turnout. Every group is different, so ask our team about the fundraising options that best fit your size and goals.
